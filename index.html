Turn 2D video into 3D on Meta Quest

Most movies, series and clips are flat. Depth Player estimates depth for every frame with a compact AI depth model that runs on the Quest's own GPU, then renders a separate image for each eye. The result is real stereoscopic 3D from ordinary 2D video, on a cinema-sized screen, without a gaming PC, streaming software or cloud processing.

Smooth motion with frame interpolation

Films are shot at 24 frames per second, which judders on a VR display. Depth Player creates the in-between frames with motion-compensated interpolation, so camera pans and action scenes play smoothly at 72, 90 or 120 Hz. Scene cuts and fast motion are detected so the picture stays clean.

A virtual cinema you control

  • Flat or curved screen, adjustable size and distance, grab and move it with the controller.
  • AMD FidelityFX Super Resolution (FSR) upscaling makes 480p and 720p video sharper.
  • Subtitle and audio track selection, aspect ratio and crop, resume where you stopped.
  • Switch between the VR cinema and a 2D window at any time.

Play videos from anywhere

  • On the headset: MP4, MKV, MOV, WebM and more, powered by libVLC.
  • Home network: DLNA / UPnP media servers (Plex, Jellyfin, Emby, NAS) and shared folders over SMB, SFTP, FTP and NFS.
  • Links and the web: open stream links, browse video sites, and watch YouTube on the big screen in VR.

Frequently asked questions

Does Depth Player need a PC?

No. 2D-to-3D conversion and frame interpolation run entirely on Meta Quest 3, Quest 3S, Quest Pro and Quest 2.

How does Depth Player convert 2D video to 3D?

A compact AI depth model (ZipDepth) estimates depth for each frame on the headset's GPU. Depth Player then renders a separate image for each eye, giving stereoscopic 3D from ordinary 2D video.

Does Depth Player make video smoother?

Yes. Motion-compensated frame interpolation plays 24 and 30 fps video at 72, 90 or 120 Hz, with protection against artifacts at scene cuts and fast motion.

Which video sources does Depth Player play?

Files on the headset (MP4, MKV, MOV, WebM and more via libVLC), DLNA/UPnP media servers such as Plex, Jellyfin and Emby, SMB, SFTP, FTP and NFS shared folders, stream links, and web video including YouTube in VR.

Can Depth Player convert Netflix or Prime Video to 3D?

No. Copy-protected streaming video can't be processed by any app. Depth Player converts your own files, network media and unprotected web video.

Which headsets are supported?

Meta Quest 3 and Quest 3S are recommended. Quest Pro and Quest 2 are supported.

Is Depth Player free?

Yes. It has no ads, no accounts and no tracking.

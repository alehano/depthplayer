Any video, in 3D.

Depth Player is a video player for Meta Quest. It turns ordinary 2D video into stereoscopic 3D in real time, right on the headset, and makes motion smooth with frame interpolation. No PC needed.

Instant 2D → 3D

An on-device depth model gives every film, series or clip natural depth on a giant virtual screen.

Smooth motion

Motion-compensated interpolation plays 24 and 30 fps video at the headset's refresh rate.

Your virtual cinema

Flat or curved screen, any size and distance, upscaling for low-resolution video.

Plays what you have

Files on the headset, media servers (DLNA), shared folders (SMB, SFTP, FTP, NFS), stream links and the web.

Support

Found a bug or have an idea? Open an issue at github.com/alehano/depthplayer/issues. Please include your headset model and what you were playing.

Common questions

How do I switch between 2D and the VR cinema?

In the 2D player, press the VR button. In VR, use the 2D button on the control panel or press B. The app remembers the mode you used last.

How do I control playback in VR?

Pull the trigger to show the control panel and point at it. A pauses and resumes, the right stick seeks, the left stick changes screen distance and size, and the grip button moves the screen.

The 3D effect is too strong or too weak.

Open the panel's settings (sliders icon) and change 3D strength.

My media server or shared folder doesn't show up.

Make sure the headset is on the same Wi-Fi network. You can also add the address by hand in Network, for example smb://192.168.1.10/Movies.

A streaming site doesn't play.

Some services only play in their own apps or protect their video. Depth Player can't show protected video in 3D.
